# Delivery Roadmap

## Sprint 0 — Governance and evidence

Confirm company naming, canonical domain, founder titles, public biographies, solution names, customer anonymization, claim owners, and approval workflow. Produce an approved content and asset inventory.

## Sprint 1 — Foundation and homepage

Make the repository installable, pass checks, finalize responsive navigation, validate both locales, complete homepage components, and deploy an internal preview.

## Sprint 2 — Conversion pages

Complete platform, three solution pages, founders, trust, resources, and contact/workshop pages. Add approved booking or CRM integration with privacy and abuse controls.

## Sprint 3 — Field proof and content operations

Publish at least three approved case or field-proof stories. Introduce a CMS only after the content workflow and approval roles are agreed. Add preview, revision, and locale-parity controls.

## Sprint 4 — Production readiness

Complete accessibility review, security header and CSP rollout, analytics/privacy review, SEO, performance optimization, legal pages, uptime monitoring, dependency scanning, backup/export, and incident ownership.

## Sprint 5 — Authenticated portal foundation

Start a separate customer-portal application with enterprise identity, tenant authorization, audit logging, and a read-only executive overview. Do not add authenticated routes to this repository.
