## Product summary

Avocado.ai is positioned as an AI-Native Security Operations & Validation Platform. SenseL connects EDR, NDR, WAF, OT, CTI, and operational data and turns them into correlated incidents, traceable evidence, control validation, and continuous improvement.

The public portal has three primary solution stories:

- Managed Security for SMB.
- Semiconductor Fab Intelligence, combining EnMS data value with an OT security expansion path.
- Healthcare Cyber Resilience, combining security operations, threat intelligence, controlled validation, remediation, and re-test.

The founders are presented as complementary leadership:

- Rain Chung — Co-Founder & CEO; company strategy, go-to-market, customer success, partnerships, and operations.
- Eric Mao, Ph.D. — Co-Founder & CTO / Chief Architect; SenseL architecture, AI-native security operations, XDR, OT/IoMT, validation, threat intelligence, and secure AI governance.
